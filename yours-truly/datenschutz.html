2. Die App

Fotos

Wenn du eine Postkarte teilst, geschieht das über das Teilen-Menü deines Geräts und die App, die du dafür auswählst (z. B. Nachrichten oder Mail). Für die Verarbeitung dort ist der jeweilige Anbieter verantwortlich.

Keine Analyse, keine Werbung

Die App enthält keine Analyse-, Tracking- oder Werbe-SDKs und verwendet keine Cookies oder vergleichbaren Technologien.

Privacy Policy for Yours Truly

Last updated: November 2026

In short: Yours Truly does not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no server of its own, no tracking, no ads and no analytics. Your photos and postcards stay on your device.

2. The app

Photos

When you design a postcard, you choose a photo from your library. The app only accesses the photos you select or have granted access to. All photos are processed exclusively on your device and are never transferred to us or any third party. You can change photo access at any time in your device settings.

Saved postcards

Your postcards are stored only locally on your device; we have no access to them. If you have device backups enabled (e.g. iCloud Backup), this data may be included in your backup, which is subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a postcard, this happens via your device’s share sheet and the app you choose (e.g. Messages or Mail). The respective provider is responsible for any processing there.

No analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and does not use cookies or similar technologies.

3. App Store and Apple

Yours Truly is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple is responsible for data processing related to the download (e.g. your Apple Account and payment processing). If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with crash reports and usage statistics in a form that does not personally identify you. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

4. Support requests by email

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to respond (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete your message once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so data may also be processed in the USA. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Just send us an email. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

We will update this privacy policy if the app or the legal requirements change. The version published here applies.
