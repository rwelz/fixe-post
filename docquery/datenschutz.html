2. Die App

Deine Dokumente

DocQuery liest nur die Dateien, die du selbst ins Fenster ziehst. Text wird mit Apples Frameworks direkt auf deinem Mac ausgelesen (PDFKit für PDFs, Vision für die Texterkennung in Bildern). Die Inhalte werden weder an uns noch an Dritte übertragen.

Zusammenfassungen und Antworten

Zusammenfassungen und Antworten erzeugt das Sprachmodell von Apple Intelligence, das lokal auf deinem Mac läuft (Foundation Models). Deine Dokumente und Fragen verlassen dabei dein Gerät nicht. DocQuery stellt keine Verbindung ins Internet her.

Gespeicherte Daten

DocQuery speichert weder deine Dokumente noch den Chatverlauf; beides wird nur im Arbeitsspeicher gehalten und ist nach dem Schließen des Fensters weg. Gespeichert wird lediglich deine Spracheinstellung, und zwar lokal auf deinem Mac.

Keine Analyse, keine Werbung

Die App enthält keine Analyse-, Tracking- oder Werbe-SDKs und verwendet keine Cookies oder vergleichbaren Technologien.

Privacy Policy for DocQuery

Last updated: November 2026

In short: DocQuery does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your documents and questions are processed only on your Mac and are never uploaded. The app has no user accounts, no server of its own, no tracking, no ads and no analytics.

This privacy policy applies to the DocQuery Mac app. Our general privacy policy applies to visits to this website.

2. The app

Your documents

DocQuery only reads the files you drag into its window. Text is extracted on your Mac using Apple’s frameworks (PDFKit for PDFs, Vision for recognizing text in images). The contents are never transferred to us or any third party.

Summaries and answers

Summaries and answers are generated by the Apple Intelligence language model that runs locally on your Mac (Foundation Models). Your documents and questions do not leave your device. DocQuery does not connect to the internet.

Stored data

DocQuery stores neither your documents nor your chat history; both are kept in memory only and are gone once you close the window. The only thing stored is your language setting, locally on your Mac.

No analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and does not use cookies or similar technologies.

3. App Store and Apple

DocQuery is distributed through Apple’s Mac App Store. Apple is responsible for data processing related to the download (e.g. your Apple Account and payment processing). If you have agreed in your system settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with crash reports and usage statistics in a form that does not personally identify you. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

We delete your message once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so data may also be processed in the USA. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details. Please don’t send us confidential documents.
